A learning philosophy

Understand.
Assess.
Reinforce. Apply.

SOLZEN's public framework describes a direction for learning: build understanding, pay attention to evidence, strengthen what needs it, and carry learning into use.

The four stages

A connected path,
not four isolated steps.

The framework expresses SOLZEN's high-level learning philosophy. It is not a description of a released product or proprietary implementation.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Start with the concept, not with memorisation. Make room to see the idea and the connections around it.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Look for evidence of what a learner understands, so the next part of learning can respond to what is needed.

  3. 03

    Reinforce

    Return to ideas that need more attention through focused opportunities to strengthen understanding.

  4. 04

    Apply

    Bring understanding into problem-solving, new situations and practical use.

Designing around learners

Clarity before complexity.

Our principles keep the focus on learning, not on technology for its own sake.

  • 01

    Concept-first

    Start from understanding rather than recall alone.

  • 02

    Human-centred

    Connect explanations with how learners naturally think.

  • 03

    Visual

    Make difficult ideas easier to see, connect and explore.

  • 04

    Measurable

    Use evidence of learning to help guide what comes next.

  • 05

    Applicable

    Move learning toward confident problem-solving and action.

Technology, kept high-level

Tools in service of learning.

SOLZEN's public direction includes visualisation, interactive learning, assessment, reinforcement and localised learning. Specific product capabilities and implementation details remain private while the work is in development.
